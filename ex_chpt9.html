    选择题

    设 \mathbf{u}=\left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}},\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\right)^T,\quad \mathbf{v}=\left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}},\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}},0\right)^T,\quad \mathbf{w}=\left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}},\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}},\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\right)^T.

    则下列判断正确的是:

    1. \mathbf{v}=\mathbf{u}
    2. \mathbf{u}=\mathbf{w}
    3. \mathbf{v}=\mathbf{w}
    4. 都不是
    5. 不知道
